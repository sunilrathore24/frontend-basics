# Brand Naming Research & Decision

_Last updated: 18 Sep 2026_

## Current decision

**Working brand name: `Rangsut` (rangsut.com)** — liked and shortlisted as of now.

- **Meaning / build:** `rang` (colour) + `sut` (thread/yarn) — a genuine compound of two real Hindi/Sanskrit textile words. Literally "colour + thread," which is exactly what a clothing brand is about.
- **Why it fits our brief:** trendy, affordable-premium, **unisex** (no gender lean), rooted in traditional Indian textile language, easy to pronounce for Indians and internationally, and has a real defensible meaning when customers/press ask "what does it mean?"
- **Domain status:** `rangsut.com` showed **no nameservers** in a DNS check → strong signal it is unregistered. **Still to confirm on GoDaddy live search (authoritative).**
- **Tagline ideas (half-English / half-Hindi):**
  - "Colour meets thread."
  - _"Rang bhi, dhaaga bhi."_

## Naming brief (criteria)

- 5–8 letters, easy to pronounce (India + international)
- Unisex — works for men's AND women's apparel
- No "fashion/clothing/wear/India" in the core name
- Unique — must NOT be confusingly close to existing brands (e.g. avoid Saajaa because it rhymes with SAADAA)
- Preferably a subtle Indian / textile / traditional root
- A hidden meaning that makes people curious to search it (like SAADAA = "simple", Bewakoof = "foolish", Suta = "thread")
- `.com` should be available (verify on GoDaddy)
- Instagram handle should be free/close
- Trademark class 25 (clothing) should be clear on IP India

## Market research — how successful Indian D2C apparel brands are named

Patterns observed among SAADAA, Suta, Bewakoof, Snitch, Nicobar, The Souled Store:

1. **A single Hindi word with a hidden meaning + attitude** — SAADAA ("simple/plain"), Bewakoof ("foolish", deliberately chosen to be unique & memorable), Suta ("thread").
2. **Short, clean, one word, easy to say** — Snitch, Nicobar.
3. **The meaning sparks curiosity** — customers search "what does it mean".

## How to choose a brand name — checklist (synthesised from branding sources)

Sources reviewed: Column Five (columnfivemedia.com), Lexicon Branding (lexiconbranding.com), SpellBrand, BigEye Agency, Fullstop360, Inkbot Design. They converge on 7 criteria:

1. **Meaningful** — communicates brand essence, conjures an image, positive emotional connection.
2. **Distinctive** — unique, stands out from competitors, not confusable.
3. **Memorable** — easy to recall after hearing once.
4. **Short & simple** — easy to say and spell; ideally 2–3 syllables, no hyphens/numbers.
5. **Available** — .com domain + social handle free, AND trademark clear (India class 25 for clothing).
6. **Extensible / future-proof** — won't box you in (don't hard-code "pants" or "women"); room to grow categories.
7. **Positive phonetics / global-safe** — sounds good aloud, no negative meaning in other languages.

## How to write the tagline (rules from research)

From Kayako, StarterStory, thebrandboy, Quora examples:

- **3–5 words**, short and memorable.
- **Emotional** and benefit-led — say what you do and why it matters.
- Reflect the brand's core promise (for us: **comfort + skin-friendly + everyday**).
- Consistent everywhere (site, packaging, ads).
- Avoid generic filler ("best quality clothes"); make it ownable.
